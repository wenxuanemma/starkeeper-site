StarKeeper · 星星罐

A star jar for good habits.

Parents tap to give a star for brushing teeth, tidying up or being brave. Stars drop into each child's jar and can be spent in the family shop on things like choosing dinner or a trip to the park. Works on iPhone, iPad and Apple Watch, synced through your own iCloud. No accounts, no ads, no leaderboards.
